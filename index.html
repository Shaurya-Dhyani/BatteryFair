Why Battery Fair?

Three problems every battery buyer faces

Buying a battery in India shouldn't feel like a gamble. Here's what we're fixing.

The Problem

Lost Warranty Cards

Paper warranty slips fade, tear, or vanish into junk drawers. When your battery dies within warranty, you're left paying full price again.

Our Solution

Digital warranty vault — scan, store, and claim. Get auto-reminders before expiry.

The Problem

No Price Transparency

Every shop quotes a different number. Without a way to compare, customers routinely overpay by ₹500–₹2,000 on a single battery.

Our Solution

Real-time price comparison across dealers nationwide so you always see the fair price.

The Problem

Unfair Old-Battery Rates

Dealers set exchange values with no standard reference. Lead prices fluctuate daily, but customers never see the real scrap rate.

Our Solution

Fair-value calculator pegged to live lead market rates — know your old battery's real worth.
