5 Essential Battery Care Tips

Proven preventive maintenance habits from certified Tumkur battery technicians.

1. Clean Terminals & Cable Clamps

Inspect lead posts every month for white or blue-green sulfate crust. Clean thoroughly with baking soda dissolved in warm water using a nylon brush. Coat posts with pure petroleum jelly (Vaseline) to prevent corrosion. Never use automotive grease, which traps dirt and increases contact resistance.

2. Check Distilled Water Level

Keep electrolyte level 10–15 mm above internal plates (or between the float indicator rings). Top up only after fully charging to prevent electrolyte boiling over due to thermal expansion. Always use genuine demineralized/distilled water.

3. Avoid Deep Discharges (<10.5V)

Never discharge a 12V lead-acid battery below 10.5 Volts (maximum 80% Depth of Discharge). When the power cuts out, recharge the battery promptly; sitting in a discharged state causes soft lead sulfate to harden into permanent insulating crystals.

4. Ensure Adequate Ventilation

Lead-acid batteries emit volatile hydrogen gas during normal charging cycles. Ensure inverter batteries are installed in an open, airy location with at least 4 inches clearance on all sides. Keep away from gas cylinders, switches, or any open flames.

5. Check Charger & Inverter Settings

Set inverter battery-type toggle correctly (Tubular vs. Flat Plate vs. SMF). Verify charging absorption voltage is calibrated between 13.8V and 14.4V (float 13.5V–13.8V). Overcharging boils away electrolyte, while undercharging causes severe plate sulfation.
